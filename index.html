Session of —

    One Democratic incumbent of the 119th Congress each day. Data from the Progressive Electoral Targeting Index.

    How to play

    Each day hides one sitting Democratic incumbent in Congress. You start with three facts: chamber, region, and years served.

    • Type a name and submit. You have 6 guesses.
    • You start with 200 points. Each hint you reveal subtracts its cost. A correct guess keeps whatever is left.
    • "Reveal all hints" opens everything and leaves you with 50 points if you then name the incumbent.
    • A new incumbent appears every day at midnight California time (Pacific Time). Past days can’t be replayed.

    Regions follow the U.S. Census: Northeast, Midwest, South, and West. PVI is the Cook Partisan Voter Index: D+11 means the district or state votes 11 points more Democratic than the nation.
